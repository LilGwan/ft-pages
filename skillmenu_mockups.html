  • Cost: small to medium (reuses the Items list).
  • Downside: the 4th and 5th skills take two presses.

C · Skill pages Your choice · D-451

  • Reaching a skill: skills 1–4 with keys 5–8; skill 5 and up: flip the page (Page Down, the right stick, or the small "1/2 ›" tag), then 5–8 again.
  • Fits the largest text size: yes. The tag takes a narrow strip on the right, so a few longer names wrap to two lines.
  • Cost: medium (the page number, the tag, the flip key, and what a number key means after a flip).

Three small choices for option C

  1. Flipping pages. (a) Page Down or Tab on the keyboard, the right stick or shoulder buttons on a controller, and clicking the tag (recommended). (b) Only the tag. (c) Your own words.
  2. Which skills are on page 1. (a) You arrange them on the team screen; new skills go to the end (recommended). (b) In the order they were unlocked, fixed. (c) The most used come first automatically.
  3. Remembering the page. (a) Each hero opens on page 1 at the start of their turn (recommended: the same keys always start in the same place). (b) Each hero stays on the page they last used.
